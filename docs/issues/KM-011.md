## Summary
Collect owner-approved, non-sensitive information necessary for public contact, Google-branding review, and an accurate privacy statement.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Owner selects a real support/privacy contact mechanism (email alias or monitored form outside the private research app).
- [ ] Confirm whether the personal/brand name or business identity should appear publicly; no inferred registrant info from WHOIS or personal records.
- [ ] Review factual claims about third-party hosting, browser cookies, identity scopes, storage, backups, retention and deletion rights against implemented product.
- [ ] Decide whether public contact link should use mailto or other provider; avoid adding a server/backend to the static site by default.
- [ ] Make the result available to KM-005 and KM-010 with no private customer details.

## Dependencies
Owner must supply/approve final contact and policy facts.

## Technical notes
Do not publish unverified commitments or fake contact addresses.
## Definition of done
- [ ] Small reviewed PR (or documented owner-only deployment action).
- [ ] Run `python scripts/check_site.py` and `python scripts/build_site.py`; record outcomes.
- [ ] No real family-history/client information, token, credential, local secret location, or private network information is introduced.
- [ ] Update `docs/CLAUDE_HANDOFF.md` and relevant documentation if the implementation decision changes.
- [ ] Don't close until acceptance criteria have actually been verified.

/ WHAT TO EXPECT

Try the research workflow, without the paperwork.

The demo is fully interactive. Browse sample clients, open and create research cases, update their status, and add journal entries, tasks and notebook notes.

  • Every record is fictional. No real client information is in the demo or reachable from it.
  • Changes are temporary. They live only in your browser tab.
  • Refresh to reset. Reloading the page brings back the original sample data.
  • No sign-in. The demo has no accounts and makes no network requests of its own.

Launch Interactive Demo ↗

The demo opens in a new tab at krunchymonkey.github.io, its own standalone address. Back to Tracing Guatemala

Real research stays private.

Neither this page nor the demo signs you in, stores your work, or connects to the privately hosted Tracing Guatemala application. The demo uses fictional sample records only.

Separate environments: The public demo and the private research application are completely separate. They share no accounts, sessions, servers or research records.

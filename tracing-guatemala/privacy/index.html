TRACING GUATEMALA

Privacy information

Draft — not a finalized privacy policy.

Tracing Guatemala is a private research workspace. This public page describes the proposed identity and data-handling approach, pending review of the actual implementation and operations.

Google account sign-in

The planned sign-in would use Google's identity service to authenticate approved accounts. The actual account fields collected, retention period, and access/revocation practices must be documented before release.

Research records

The intended research application is locally hosted and separate from this public website. This website does not provide access to private client research records.

Before launch

The operator must verify deployed behavior, fill in privacy contact and applicable retention/deletion details, and approve a complete policy. This draft should not be submitted as a finalized Google OAuth privacy policy.
